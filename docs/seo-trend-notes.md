# SEO trend notes used for Fun Ground

Source files: four user-supplied worldwide search-trend CSV exports dated 19–20 October 2026. These are a short snapshot, not a stable keyword forecast.

## Relevant broad intent

The top-search exports repeatedly include natural variants of “free online games”, “online games”, “play games online”, and “games online free”. The rising-search exports include broad intents such as “snake game”, “math games”, “classroom games”, and “unblocked games”. These are treated as research context only; the site copy uses plain, readable wording such as “free online games” and “browser games”, not repeated keyword strings.

## Terms intentionally not used as target keywords

Brand or platform queries (for example Poki, CrazyGames, ABCya, Cool Math Games, and Google Games), unrelated sports/entertainment queries, casino queries, and queries that do not accurately describe the actual Fun Ground catalogue are not inserted into page copy or metadata. Do not imply affiliation with other brands.

## Implementation guidance

- Write distinct page titles and descriptions based on each actual game's name, category, and available description.
- Keep headings useful to players; avoid keyword stuffing and hidden keyword lists.
- Do not claim measured popularity or verified release dates because the supplied game records do not contain play counts or trustworthy publication timestamps.
- Revisit keyword choices using longer-term Search Console data after the real domain is verified and receives traffic.
